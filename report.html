Methodology and limitations

  • SQLite joined the product catalog to 9,994 U.S. sales lines from 5,009 customers. The queries use customer destination and order date and exclude 200 Canadian lines.
  • Gross profit equals sales less manufacturing cost on every line. Gross margin divides total gross profit by total revenue.
  • U.S. order IDs contain product suffixes. The dashboard counts distinct source IDs. Checkout transaction counts remain unverified.
  • The product catalog classifies Fizzy Lifting Drinks as Other. Six sales lines label it Sugar. Division totals follow the catalog.
  • Maven documents 2024 division targets, but their geographic coverage is unspecified. The dashboard omits target attainment. Source ship dates fall in 2026–2030, so the analysis uses order dates only.
